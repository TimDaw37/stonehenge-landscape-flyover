Stonehenge landscape
Fly anywhere. WS/↑↓ forward and back, AD sideways, ←→ turn (or drag the compass round; click it or press N to face north), E/R up, Q/F down, Shift faster, L labels, P plan view (with alignment rays).
Mouse: left-drag grabs and moves the landscape, right-drag (or Ctrl/Alt+drag) looks around, middle-drag (or Shift+right-drag) orbits the grabbed point, wheel moves toward the pointer, double-click flies there. Plan view: drag pans, right-drag turns. Touch: one finger moves the landscape, pinch zooms, twist turns, two-finger drag tilts.
Drag the ground to move it, right-drag to look, wheel to zoom, double-click to fly there. Drag the compass (top right) round to turn the view; click it or press N to face north. L labels, P plan view. The pad (bottom right) flies and shows the keys.
